01 / WHO THIS IS FOR

When software sits inside the operation.

Managed Application is for systems whose reliability, workflows, transactions or data directly affect customers, staff, revenue or core service delivery.

Typical examples include large portals, SaaS applications, internal systems, booking and billing platforms, workflow systems and multi-user operational products.

02 / BUSINESS-CRITICAL RESPONSIBILITY

What Expert Systems Technology may operate

Application services, APIs, databases, production infrastructure, telemetry, deployments, release workflows, backup/restore and contract-defined incident operations.

Customer responsibility

Business policy, authorised data use, internal process ownership, timely decisions, user administration and responsibilities assigned by the contract.

Capacity & incidents

Capacity planning, service levels, escalation, response and recovery are scoped against actual criticality rather than assumed from a package name.

Security & reliability

Identity, permissions, environment isolation, observability, backup and recovery are designed around the application’s data and operating risk.

05 / OWNERSHIP & OPERATION

The contract defines the model.

Managed Application normally uses contract-defined licensing and operation terms. It does not mean that a customer never owns anything, and it does not promise full ownership.

Ownership and intellectual property are contractual. A prospect requiring a fully bespoke owned system needs a separate discussion about delivery and ongoing operation.
